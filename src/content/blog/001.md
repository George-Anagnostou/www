Let there be content!

At long last, the site is alive!
After many iterations, countless nights of procrastination, and endless back-and-forth about formatting, purpose, and style, I've finally brought this site to life!
It's about time!

Have I made any strong decisions about any of those things that held me up for so long?
Yes!
I've decided that they are all much less important than getting something, *anything*, out there into the world.
So, what is the purpose of this site?
Of this blog?
I'm not sure yet, but that's also why I am excited to finally get this off the ground.
For now, this site will be part portfolio piece and part time capsule;
you will find some [interesting projects](/projects) that I've been working on, as well as all of the writings I've decided were adequate to include in [my blog](/writing).

This introductory post (and my site!) wouldn't be complete without a bow of immense gratitude to [Christian Anagnostou](https://www.christiancodes.co/), to whom I owe all of this. Thank you, Christian.
