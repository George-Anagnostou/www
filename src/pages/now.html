Now

What has my attention lately, from San Francisco.

Updated September 26, 2026

Software, and the people around it

I work in finance, but software is the thing I keep coming back to. I'm learning more about the academic software communities in San Francisco and getting to know people who like to go deep.

I love teaching. At work, I've started a Python and AI training series for colleagues. Building something is fun; helping someone understand how it works is fun, too.

My side projects include GPTees and Bookmarks. This site is getting a little more personal, too.

Too many tabs

I read an absurd number of tech blogs: the state of Rust, kernel races and locks in Solaris at Sun Microsystems, the joy of writing Lisp. Apparently, there is no minimum age for a debugging story to become interesting again.

I also finished two books by Brian Kernighan, Millions, Billions, Zillions and Understanding the Digital World. I wrote a little about them.

Outside

I enjoy endurance sports, especially marathons and triathlons. This year included the LA Marathon, Escape from Alcatraz, and the SF Marathon on July 26. Long runs and long swims have become a pretty good reason to close the laptop.

A little further afield

I want to travel more, and bring lessons from abroad back home with me. I love diving deep into history, and learning languages is part of the fun: I speak German and am learning Greek.

Language Transfer has been a good companion for Greek. I really like Mihalis' approach to teaching.
