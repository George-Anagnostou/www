What has my attention lately.

Updated September 26, 2026

Software, and the people around it

I work in finance, but software is the thing I keep coming back to. I'm learning more about the academic software communities in San Francisco, such as Papers We Love, and getting to know people who are endlessly curious.

I love teaching. At work, I've started a Python and AI training series for colleagues. Giving someone AI or code is nice; teaching them how to use AI and write code is priceless.

My side projects include GPTees and Bookmarks. This site is getting a little more personal, too.

What I'm reading

Besides reading way too much on lobste.rs, I also like reading books. I recently finished two books by Brian Kernighan, Millions, Billions, Zillions and Understanding the Digital World. I wrote a little about them, and I'd recommend you read them, too. I'm currently (slowly) working my way through SICP and Brian Harvey's 61A lectures.

Outside

I enjoy endurance sports, especially marathons and triathlons. This year included the LA Marathon, Escape from Alcatraz, and the SF Marathon. These endurance events have been a great way to stay in shape and quiet the mind.

It's all Greek to me...

I've made it a point to travel more. I love diving deep into history, picking up culture, and making spontaneous connections. Learning the language and being a bit of a fool is part of the fun! I speak German and I am learning Greek, but my trip to Peru has me reconsidering Spanish...

Language Transfer has been a good companion for Greek. I really like Mihalis' approach to teaching.
