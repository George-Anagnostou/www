About

Bay Area native. Four years in wealth management. I write software because I like making things that work.

Background

I grew up in Silicon Valley — literally, not as a metaphor. Being around builders early on probably shaped how I think about problems: if something is broken, make something that fixes it.

Finance

After school I joined J.P. Morgan, where I've spent four years in wealth management working with venture capital clients — funds, portfolio companies, founders, and investors. My specialty is distributions in kind: transferring non-cash assets from funds to their limited partners.

It's precise, cross-functional work. Legal, operations, and client teams all need to stay aligned.

Software

I've always written code. What changed was building tools at work — a Python webapp to automate distributions in kind, then a form prefill tool deployed division-wide. Software that other people actually use felt different from projects only I touched.

Since then: a trivia game in Go, Advent of Code in Rust, this site's static generator in TypeScript. Different languages, same habit.

What I'm looking for

Work at the boundary of finance and technology — where knowing both is useful, not decorative. I'm not trying to pick a side. I'm looking for the work that needs both.
