# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

First-time visitors should get to know George as a person. Visitors interested in his writing, projects, or professional background can follow clear links to those subjects.

## Product Purpose

A personal home on the internet: an introduction, a place to write and share projects, and a way to make connections. The homepage is a compact personal directory, not a professional pitch.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Keep the existing Bun static generator, Markdown publishing, clean routes, resume PDF, and Vercel deployment. Preserve published post URLs. Keep About, Now, Writing, Projects, and Experience easy to find. Do not invent personal events or claims.

## Brand Commitments

George wants the site to stay light and fun. He lives in San Francisco and works in finance, but software is his passion. He loves teaching, history, endurance sports, and travel. His reading ranges from Rust and Solaris debugging to Lisp. He is getting to know academic software communities in San Francisco and wants to bring lessons from travel back home.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing personal essays, two blog posts, project descriptions and screenshots, a headshot, a race photo, and the resume in `src/static/files/`. The user confirmed that council service began in 2023; the resume corroborates it. Preserve the existing headshot on About; the chosen compact directory does not require a homepage portrait.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the person and their curiosity.
- Let visitors choose how deeply to explore.
- Favor specific, natural language over promotional copy.
- Keep reading and navigation simple on phones and keyboards.
- Make publishing predictable and failures visible.
